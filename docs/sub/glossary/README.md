# Glossary

Status: draft

This glossary defines working Ontoverse terms.

Definitions may change as the framework is refined.

Unless explicitly stated otherwise, Ontoverse-specific terms are proposed conceptual terms, not established scientific terminology. They should be read as working definitions for a pre-formal framework.

## Absolute Baseline Metric

Type: proposed Ontoverse term  
Status: speculative working definition

A shared conceptual zero-point for metric interpretation.

It represents an abstract reference state with no mass, no gravitational distortion, and no meaningful causal processing density. It is not treated as a living timeline state because without mass, energy, or state changes there are no events to actualize.

## Branch Baseline Metric

Type: proposed Ontoverse term  
Status: speculative working definition

The average nominal metric state of one currently distinguishable historical strand.

Before a history-class split, several admissible complete continuations may share one represented baseline. After a split, child strands may develop distinct baselines.

## Branch Time Rate

Type: proposed Ontoverse term  
Status: speculative working definition

The default temporal character of a historical strand relative to frontal time.

It is associated with the strand baseline metric, causal processing density, and effective rate of significant strand-local transitions.

## Causal Processing Density

Type: proposed Ontoverse term  
Status: speculative working definition

The proposed amount of local state coordination, constraint, interaction bookkeeping, or physical processing load that must be maintained in a region relative to frontal-time slices.

It is distinct from **Temporal Density**, which counts significant event-nodes per frontal-time interval.

The current gravity interpretation explores whether higher causal processing density could reduce the effective rate of significant transitions and therefore reduce local-time accumulation relative to frontal time. This relation is speculative, not a defined physical law.

## Closed Time Loop

Type: proposed Ontoverse term  
Status: speculative working definition

A globally self-consistent causal structure in which a later departure enters isolated past-directed transit and returns to an earlier state of a loop-compatible historical strand.

The departure event does not itself create a branch. If loop-compatible and loop-free continuations require different earlier states, their history class splits at the earliest required state distinction, commonly the reintegration event in the minimal model.

## Compatibility Channel

Type: proposed Ontoverse term  
Status: working definition

An access structure in which mutually compatible states, records, and observers can interact.

Several globally distinct historical strands may share a compatibility channel when their locally accessible states are equivalent under the chosen description.

## Convergent Channel

Type: proposed Ontoverse term  
Status: working definition

A compatibility channel entered by more than one globally distinct historical strand.

Convergence means local equivalence under a chosen description, not global historical identity and not erasure of prior divergence.

## Dark-Matter-Like Effect

Type: observational analogy / Ontoverse comparison term  
Status: external comparison target

A gravitational effect resembling phenomena usually attributed to dark matter, such as additional gravitational influence not explained by visible matter alone.

In Ontoverse this term is used cautiously as a comparison target, not as a claim that dark matter has been explained.

## Dense Cluster

Type: proposed Ontoverse term  
Status: working definition

A local interval or region where significant event-nodes are concentrated more tightly than in surrounding parts of a historical strand or history-space.

## Divergence Node

Type: proposed Ontoverse term  
Status: working definition

The earliest significant location at which one previously represented **History Equivalence Class** must separate into multiple incompatible current state descriptions.

A divergence node is therefore interpreted as a **first required state distinction**, not necessarily as the instant when all possible future universes are created.

A Closed Time Loop departure is not a divergence node merely because the traveler leaves ordinary forward historical synchronization. In the minimal loop model, reintegration may instead be the divergence point between loop-compatible and loop-free histories because that is where their physical states first differ.

## Effective Quantum Transition Rate

Type: proposed Ontoverse placeholder  
Status: open formalization task

A placeholder for the effective rate at which significant quantum transitions become event-nodes along a historical strand relative to frontal time.

It may eventually depend on Hamiltonian evolution, interaction strength, coupling between states, density of available states, decoherence-related processes, causal processing load, or dimensionless physical relations involving the quantum of action.

## Event-Density Dark Sector Conjecture

Type: Ontoverse conjecture  
Status: open formalization task

A conjecture that some dark-matter-like gravitational effects may correspond to an effective metric contribution from undetected causal-processing-density structures and their influence on local transition rates.

The conjecture does not claim that dark matter has been explained. It requires a rigorous bridge from causal processing density to transition rate, local time-rate gradients, metric deviation, and observable gravitational behavior.

## Event-Node

Type: proposed Ontoverse term  
Status: open definition

A significant transition point in a historical strand.

A strand need not contain an event-node at every frontal-time slice. Its state may persist or change below the threshold represented by an event-node.

The exact criteria for significance are not yet formalized.

## Frontal Time

Type: proposed Ontoverse term  
Status: speculative working definition

A proposed global ordering parameter `F` for the unfolding of the history bundle.

A value of frontal time corresponds conceptually to a global cross-section of all currently distinguishable history classes rather than to a timestamp on only one strand.

The current maximum `F_max` represents the latest actualized global slice in the shown model state.

## Frontal-Time Slice

Type: proposed Ontoverse term  
Status: speculative working definition

The global history-bundle cross-section associated with one frontal-time value `F`.

It is written conceptually as `S(F)` and contains the current state of every represented distinguishable history class at that global ordering value.

The class representation may still compress many admissible complete continuations that do not yet require different current states.

## Frontal Time Plane

Type: visual Ontoverse term  
Status: working definition

The ruby visual plane representing a frontal-time slice.

When the plane represents the current maximal slice `S(F_max)`, it is the present frontier and realized strands normally terminate there.

A retrospective causal-structure diagram may instead use an earlier selected slice `S(F_ref)` as a reference plane inside already described history. In that case realized structure may appear on both sides of the plane.

## Global Consistency Constraint

Type: proposed Ontoverse structural rule  
Status: open formalization task

The requirement that every represented current history state belong to at least one globally self-consistent complete continuation.

The rule may force a history class to split earlier than a purely local forward process would predict, while still allowing several mutually incompatible but individually self-consistent continuations.

## Historical Multiplicity

Type: proposed Ontoverse term  
Status: working definition

The number of globally distinct historical origins or strands represented inside a convergent channel or local-equivalence structure.

## Historical Strand

Type: proposed Ontoverse term  
Status: working definition

The visible or working representation of one currently distinguishable **History Equivalence Class** across frontal-time slices.

A historical strand should not automatically be interpreted as one fully enumerated universe that existed separately from every other continuation from the earliest state.

## Historical Trajectory

Type: compatibility term  
Status: working definition

A general path through history-space consisting of mutually compatible states, records, and interactions.

In current Ontoverse terminology, **Historical Strand** is preferred when the text specifically means one currently distinguishable history-class representation inside the history bundle.

## History Bundle

Type: proposed Ontoverse term  
Status: speculative working definition

The branching set of currently distinguishable historical strands progressing in the same global frontal-time direction.

A strand may represent many admissible complete continuations while those continuations remain state-equivalent at the current model resolution.

The term is a structural analogy and does not claim formal equivalence to a mathematical fiber bundle.

## History Equivalence Class

Type: proposed Ontoverse term  
Status: speculative working definition

A set of admissible complete histories that still require the same modeled current physical state, records, and causal description at a given frontal-time value.

The conceptual relation is written:

```text
H1 ~F H2
```

when histories `H1` and `H2` do not yet require separate current state descriptions at frontal-time value `F`.

## History-Space

Type: proposed Ontoverse term  
Status: open formalization task

The structured space of admissible complete histories and their relations in the Ontoverse framework.

The currently represented part of history-space is modeled as a compressed **History Bundle** of distinguishable history classes.

## Light-Path Analogy

Type: interpretational analogy  
Status: bounded analogy

A conceptual analogy inspired by path-based explanations of light propagation.

In Ontoverse it is used only as a metaphor for thinking about experienced history as one compatible local route through a wider space of admissible paths.

## Local Metric Deviation

Type: proposed Ontoverse term  
Status: speculative working definition

A local departure from a historical-strand baseline metric.

Examples may include regions associated with planets, stars, black holes, dense matter distributions, low-density regions, or gravitational waves. Such deviations may change causal processing density, local transition rates, local time-rate gradients, and possible trajectories.

## Local Time

Type: proposed Ontoverse term  
Status: speculative working definition

Time experienced along a particular historical strand.

In Ontoverse, local time is associated with the accumulation of significant event-nodes across frontal-time slices.

## Local Time-Rate Gradient

Type: proposed Ontoverse term  
Status: speculative working definition

A spatial difference in local time rate across neighboring regions.

In the gravity interpretation, this gradient is the conceptual bridge between local time-rate slowdown and curved possible trajectories.

## Minimal Distinguishability Principle

Type: proposed Ontoverse structural rule  
Status: speculative working definition

The rule that admissible histories remain represented together while their current modeled states are equivalent and split into separate history classes only when global consistency requires distinct current physical, record, or causal states.

This is currently a conceptual compression principle, not an established physical law.

## Observer Access

Type: proposed Ontoverse term  
Status: working definition

The constraint that an observer can interact only with states, records, and other observers compatible with the observer's local history.

A global frontal-time slice may contain many history-class states even though a local observer accesses only one compatible strand or local-equivalence channel.

## Parameter-Enriched Interval

Type: proposed Ontoverse term  
Status: speculative working definition

The interval between reintegration and departure in a Closed Time Loop during which the loop-compatible historical strand requires an expanded state description including the reintegrated traveler and its causal consequences.

The term does not currently imply extra energy, mass, entropy, or another conserved physical quantity.

## Past-Directed Transit

Type: proposed Ontoverse term  
Status: speculative working definition

A temporally isolated causal path connecting a later departure event to an earlier reintegration event within a Closed Time Loop.

The transit path does not by itself represent a new ordinary historical strand. Any history-class split is determined by the earliest required state distinction between admissible complete histories.

## Planck-Action Hypothesis

Type: historical Ontoverse formulation  
Status: superseded / refined

An earlier formulation that used Planck's constant, understood as a quantum of action, as a candidate clue for formalizing temporal density.

This has been refined into the quantum transition rate conjecture: the quantum of action remains relevant background, but temporal density is framed as depending more directly on an effective rate of significant transitions relative to frontal time.

## Potential Branch

Type: visual Ontoverse term  
Status: working definition

A dotted or auxiliary path used in diagrams to represent an admissible incompatible continuation that is not yet shown as a separately realized historical strand in that visual slice.

A potential branch may remain compressed inside a History Equivalence Class until a current state distinction requires explicit splitting.

A Closed Time Loop transit path is not a potential branch merely because it leaves the ordinary forward-flow strand.

## Quantum Transition Rate Conjecture

Type: Ontoverse conjecture  
Status: open formalization task

A conjecture that temporal density may depend on an effective rate of significant quantum transitions along a historical strand relative to frontal time.

In this conjecture, `h` or `hbar` is not a direct density measure. Effective transition dynamics may instead involve relations such as `H_eff / hbar_eff`, interaction strengths, available states, decoherence, causal processing load, and other physical structure requiring formalization.

## Temporal Density

Type: proposed Ontoverse term  
Status: speculative working definition

The proposed frequency of significant event-nodes per unit of frontal time along a historical strand.

Under the frontal-time-slice interpretation, higher temporal density means more significant transitions over the same `ΔF`; lower temporal density means fewer.

A history class that later splits may share one represented density description before divergence and separate density descriptions afterward.

## Temporal Density Pattern

Type: visual Ontoverse term  
Status: working definition

A visual pattern describing how significant event-nodes are distributed along a historical strand or region across frontal-time intervals.

Current patterns include sparse, sparse-to-dense, dense-to-sparse, burst cluster, uniform medium, and mixed density.
